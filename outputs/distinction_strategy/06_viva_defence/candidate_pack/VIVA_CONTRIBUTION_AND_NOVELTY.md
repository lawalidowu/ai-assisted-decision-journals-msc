# Contribution and novelty defence

## Direct contribution statement (60s)

**Methodological:** a governed review process that separates generation from acceptance.  
**Empirical:** shows that strongly supported wording can still be the wrong journal artefact (No × High; phase1-082).  
**Technical:** stable IDs, frozen 414-entry journal, claim lineage, deterministic examiner inspection.  
**Governance:** keeps source evidence, automated checks and human judgement visibly separate.

## What is genuinely novel?

Not a new foundation model. Novelty is the **operational separation of constructs** on a public-inquiry case with frozen evidence and examiner-reproducible artefacts.

| Classic IE | LLM candidates plus faithfulness / validity / framework layers |

## Unsafe overclaims to avoid

- “We produced the Inquiry’s decision catalogue.”  
- “This is production-ready.”  
- “Our model is SOTA.”  
- “Results generalise to all inquiries / models.”

## Strongest one-sentence finding

Traceable, high-evidence wording can still fail journal validity — so automated quote support must not be treated as acceptance.
