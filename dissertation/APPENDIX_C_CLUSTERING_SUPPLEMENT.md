# Appendix C: Clustering supplement

This appendix supplements Chapter 4 Section 4.6. Exploratory clustering organised all 414 candidates in the fixed reference dataset into 20 groups for navigation. Labels are heuristic and do not validate journal membership or assign JEE/Decision Quality categories.

An earlier dense cluster-composition graphic was too small to read at dissertation page size and is replaced here by readable cluster tables. Cluster membership, sizes and heuristic labels are unchanged.

## C.1 Largest heuristic themes

| Cluster ID | Heuristic label | n | % of corpus | Example candidate (shortened) |
|------------|-----------------|--:|------------:|-------------------------------|
| 4 | UK Government COVID-19 Response Strategy | 140 | 34% | The Coronavirus action plan was published, outlining the strategic approach of the UK Government. |
| 8 | Equity and Health Disparities | 63 | 15% | An ambitious package of measures to prevent transmission among disproportionately impacted groups was agreed. |
| 3 | Crisis Coordination and Governance | 59 | 14% | Responsibility for delivery during the crisis should have been broadened to other parts of government earlier. |
| 2 | Support for Vulnerable Populations | 42 | 10% | Free school meals will be made available to children during holiday and other periods. |
| 0 | Cabinet Office Governance and Response | 25 | 6% | Ministerial implementation groups and the Covid-19 Taskforce were established to address the crisis. |

Group size provides a starting point for navigation. It does not establish policy importance or journal validity.

## C.3 Inquiry-procedure and low-support groups

Examples of smaller groups that capture inquiry procedure rather than pandemic-policy substance include Hearing Schedule and Witnesses (n = 4) and Inquiry Process and Documentation (n = 24). Twelve low-support groups (n ≤ 5), including singleton outliers, are retained in the clustering report and are not discussed individually in Chapter 4. Clustering does not delete procedural entries; it surfaces them for human review. Full cluster membership fields remain in the fixed reference dataset and clustering report for audit.
